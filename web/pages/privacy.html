Privacy

What Scoreline keeps about you, and why.

What we store

  • Your account. A passkey account has no name or email: it's called "Scoreline user". If you continue with Google, we keep the name, email address and picture Google shares.
  • Your passkeys' public keys, with each one's name and the date you added it. The private key never leaves your device or password manager.
  • Your settings: the teams and leagues you follow and your other choices, so they follow you to every device you sign in on.
  • A session cookie that keeps you signed in. It's the only cookie Scoreline sets.
  • Usage events: which pages are opened and which features are used, counted by Scoreline itself, and for visits to this site the referring site, the country and the kind of device. We store no IP addresses.

What we don't do

No third-party trackers, ad pixels or analytics scripts. We don't sell or share what we store.

Some data loads in your browser straight from where it lives: team logos and player photos from the sports feeds' image servers, and some scores and odds from ESPN and Polymarket. Those services see the request as they would on their own sites.

Where it's kept

On Cloudflare, which runs Scoreline.

Deleting it

Delete your account in Settings → Account. That removes your account, passkeys, settings and the usage events tied to you, at once. A passkey stays in your password manager until you remove it there.
