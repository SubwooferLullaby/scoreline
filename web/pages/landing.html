Live scores, told play by play.

Scoreline follows the games you care about and shows each one as it happens: every pitch, drive and shot in order, on a small animated field, with the score, the timeline and the box score beside it.

Get Started makes a passkey account: Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello or your password manager. No password, no email.

What's in it

Every play, in order
Each game page runs a short delay, so plays arrive in sequence and never jump.
A small animated field
Snaps, pitches and shots replayed as they happened, with the key player tagged.
Every league in one list
The leagues you follow, live, with the situation under each score.
The whole game
The timeline, the box score, win probability, schedules, standings and brackets.
Yours on every device
Your teams and settings follow you wherever you sign in.
Alerts, if you want them
A notification when your team scores, while Scoreline is open in a tab.

How it works

Scores come from public sports feeds. A play usually reaches Scoreline within a minute of happening. It runs in any browser and installs to your phone's home screen like an app.

Cost

Free. No ads, no trackers: the only analytics are Scoreline's own, and they never leave its database.
