Scores, plays, schedules, standings and odds can arrive late, be wrong or be missing. Don't rely on Scoreline for anything that matters, such as a bet.

Don't abuse Scoreline, scrape it, overload it or get around its limits. We may end accounts that do.

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Account. See Privacy for what that removes.
